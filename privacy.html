MangoWolfe Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

MangoWolfe Studio is an internal tool used by MangoWolfe, an art shop on Etsy, to publish Pins about its own products to its own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public and no one other than the shop owner can authorise it.

What the tool accesses

  • The MangoWolfe Pinterest business account's own boards and Pins (read and create).
  • Basic analytics for that account's own Pins.

It does not access any other Pinterest user's data, and it does not collect personal data from visitors to Pinterest or to the Etsy shop.

What is stored

The only data stored is the OAuth access and refresh tokens for the MangoWolfe account, kept in an encrypted local file on the owner's computer. No Pinterest data is copied to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's connected-apps settings. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message MangoWolfe on Etsy.
